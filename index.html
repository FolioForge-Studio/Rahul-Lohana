Selected Work

Things he has been building 02
01

NMIMS Laundry

Android app prototype · Kotlin · Jetpack Compose

A hostel laundry management app built for the SVKM’s NMIMS Shirpur campus. It replaces paper laundry slips with a digital flow: log clothes by category, get a QR-coded digital laundry slip for desk pickup, and track every batch through each stage - Collected, Washing, Drying, Ironing, Ready.

It also manages the monthly plan (₹500 / 75 garments) with invoices and PDF receipts, plus a Staff Desk side. Runs on built-in demo data with Google sign-in, biometric unlock and offline Room storage.

02

AURA Weather

Android app · Kotlin · Weather & AQI

A weather app for Android with live conditions - temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind and pressure - plus a full air-quality view: US and European AQI, key atmospheric pollutants and plain-language health guidance.

Save multiple cities and switch between them instantly. Originally written in Kotlin (full code on GitHub); an installable APK build is available for anyone to try.

03

NourishAI

Web app · HTML · CSS · JavaScript

A diet-planning assistant that runs entirely in the browser - no sign-up, no backend. It has a Diet Analyzer covering about 110 Indian foods, a Recipe Generator with 20 healthy recipes, and a gamified rewards system with points, badges and daily streaks, plus a light/dark mode.

Built with vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript with a rule-based recommendation engine.

04

Catalyst JEE

Android app · Kotlin · Jetpack Compose

A JEE preparation app for Android. It packs a previous-year question bank with step-by-step solutions - JEE Main sessions from 2020 onward and official JEE Advanced papers - plus full-length 180-minute timed mock papers.

It also builds high-yield chapter study notes, flags common exam traps, and tracks practice with analytics, bookmarks and day streaks.
